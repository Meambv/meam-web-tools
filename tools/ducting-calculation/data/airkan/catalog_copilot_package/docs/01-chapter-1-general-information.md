Introduction

Sir, Madame,

Herewith we gladly present you our latest catalogue.
The new prices are valid for deliveries per 01-03-2017.

By means of the main and sub tabs you will find the right articles very quickly.
We have also considerably simplified the hard conversion for inox304, inox316 or seawater-proof aluminium pieces (ALU
ALMG3).

The prices of the tubes are now printed next to them and for the fittings you only have to use 1 single factor per raw
material. Thanks to our price-quality-service, improved production method, investments in new machines, use of
seawater-proof aluminium, .... we are the market leader in Belgium. Our impact on European level is also very large.

Misprints reserved. Drawings and illustrations are not binding.
Airkan reserves the right to change the material, information and prices without prior notice.

Not all materials delivered by Airkan have a zinc quality Z275, this only concerns the specials and assembly materials.
These are: suspension brackets, DGR grills, METU inspection covers and inlay materials.
