Technical information

Materials:
-  GALVA sheet metal - Others: INOX304 - INOX316 - ALU ALMG3 - BACTE

Material thickness:
-  0,75mm to 1,5 mm, depending on the air pressure and the duct dimensions
-  low pressure - 0 - 50 mm. wg. - medium pressure - 51 - 100 mm. wg. - high pressure - 101 - 200 mm. wg.

Duct dimensions:
-  All dimensions possible conform to the Eurovent prescriptions.

Air leakage and construction:
- As per Eurovent prescription class A - B or C
- dossier 105 and LUKA. (certificates of duct from 0,75 to 1,5 mm metal thichness can be consulted at our offices).

Frames and intallations:
-  By means of drive slips (not standard), bolts and nuts, clamps.

Suspension materials:
- Suspension angles -  Mousse or shock absorbing rubbers - galvanized threaded rod with length 1m-2m

Inside insulation:
- Standard thickness: 25 mm, air velocity 12 m/sec., insulation protected by a neoprene impregnated sheet on the
fiberglass.
- All insulated ducts are provided with a shett metal angle in the 4 corners.
- Use of incombustible adhesives.
- Special fixing pins are provided all 500 mm. on ducts with important dimensions or fixations profiles.
- Sound attenuation and insulation specs on demand.

Length of standard ducts:
-  The construction length of standard ducts depends on the type of frame

Adjustable ducts:
-  Some ducts are delivered with a removable frame. This makes it possible to adjust the length of the duct at installation
site. (Those ducts are indicated with the letter "R".)

Tolerance:
-  Accuracy on length of ducts: 5 mm; on dimensions: 2 mm; on α: 3°, on material thickness as per DIN norms.

Attention

If you don’t give information on the airtightness class and pressure, we offer standard class A low pressure.

Technical information(2)

Important remark:
As a deafult the new TDC20 and TDC30 frames are immediately folded to the duct. This improves the airtightness and the
quality of the duct. The loose frame remains E20 and E30 and can be coupled onto the new TDC20 and TDC30 frames. The
standard dimensions are then: cf. table 1.

Attention : The loose frame is not ready for installation. The frame has to be correctly installed and masticated on-site.

Certificates:
Certificates exist on all constructed ducts for quality, air leakage, max. pressure, etc... They are controlled and edited by
an official organisation. All certificates can be consulted in our offices.

Fabrication of the ducts:
Ducts are made from coils of galv. steel with the use of an automatic ductmachine. With this procedure, the greater part of
the ducts are made out of one single sheet, and already equipped with excellent reinforcing. Advantages: high rigidity of
the duct through "Z" reinforcement. Very hightightness, only one seam per duct (from 150 to 1250 mm)(max.
circumference duct 4000mm). Cf. table 2 Pittsburg side seams and Snaplock side seams are according to standardization
the best seams with regard to solidity and airtightness.

Vergacol:
Upon your request we can apply a sealer on the longitudinal seams and on the frames. This is no ordinary sealer, but
Vergacol, an incombustiblen, non hardening saler. (Characteristics and certificates upon request).

Duct side-reinforcing:
In standard production, provided on each duct with metal thickness of 0,75 to 1,5 mm. This applies also for ducts with
negative air pressure, as this kind of reinforcing is independant of the kind of pressure.

Important remark

Definition of a rectangular duct:
- minimum dimension ≥ 150MM
- contour ≥ 600
- standard length 916, 1166 and 1416 in case of TDC20
- standard length 892, 1142 and 1392 in case of TDC30
- outside standard lengths the price of the fittings will be calculated. For ducts > 0,9 and material <> GALVA the price will
be calculated with standard length 1516.
- in GALVA 0,7 and 0,9

You have to pay an additional cost if you want:
- E30-frame instead of E20-frame = + 2,5% per frame
- A40-frame instead of E20-frame = + 2,5% per frame
- A40-frame instead of E30-frame = + 2,5% per frame
- if default frame is TDC , and E-frame is asked, then + 2,5% per frame
- class C = + 5% with regard to class B
- ducts for high pressure = additional cost according to sheet thickness (Cf. table ‘high pressure‘ pag.155) + 10%

- We recommend to use for Class C: TDC20 with angle in mousse MH20 or TDC30 with angle in mousse MH30.

- Drive slips type SR, are not applicable with TDC20.
